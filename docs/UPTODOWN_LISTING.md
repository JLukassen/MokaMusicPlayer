## Full description

Moka Music Player is an independent Android audio player for listeners who want high-quality playback and more control over their sound. Browse your device's music library, personalize playback with DSP, and optionally connect to a Navidrome or compatible Subsonic server.

**Local library and playback**
- Browse music by tracks, albums and artists.
- Play supported music formats, including FLAC and WAV.
- Create queues, use shuffle and explore available source-audio information.
- Enjoy offline local playback without creating an account.

**Advanced sound controls**
- Adjust the equalizer and native audio processing settings.
- Use supported convolution impulse responses and ViPER-DDC headphone correction files.
- Save output-device sound profiles and tailor the way your headphones sound.
- Use optional loudness normalization and local loudness analysis.

**Your own streaming library**
- Connect to a compatible HTTPS Navidrome/Subsonic music server.
- Browse hosted tracks, artists, albums and genres.
- Request original/lossless streaming where supported.
- Mix local tracks and hosted music in queues and shuffle.
- Optionally remember the server login with encrypted storage protected by Android Keystore.

**Audio information without inflated claims**

Moka reports the playback format and available output details rather than automatically labeling every stream bit-perfect. Exact format support, latency, audio processing and output capabilities depend on the device, file and connected hardware.

Moka does not include music or streaming subscriptions. Network services are optional and require your own compatible server. Your local audio remains on your device.

Built by Yoo Shastan, an independent Android app studio.
