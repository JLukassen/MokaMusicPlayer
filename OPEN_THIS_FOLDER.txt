Moka Music Player 4.0.0-beta01

Open this folder in Android Studio or build from terminal.

Recommended local test build:
  ./gradlew clean :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:assembleRelease --no-build-cache

This Beta candidate is local-first. Subscription streaming has been removed from the product path.
Key areas: Unicode library, album/artist artwork, optional MusicBrainz enrichment, native FIR/IIR/DDC/convolution,
automatic headroom, look-ahead limiting, Track/Album normalization and offline loudness analysis.

Do not publish this candidate until the physical-device gates in BETA_CHECKLIST.md have been exercised.
